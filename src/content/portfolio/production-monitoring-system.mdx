> **Project Vision:** Transforming manual production logs into a unified digital ecosystem to enforce operational limits and unlock data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 The Business Challenge

Before the implementation of **Production Monitoring System**, the production facility relied heavily on fragmented, manual record-keeping. This traditional approach created significant bottlenecks in scaling operations.

### Critical Pain Points:
* **Manual Quota Enforcement:** Production limits (daily and monthly) were tracked on paper, making it impossible to prevent over-capacity in real-time.
* **Delayed Reporting:** Daily production totals were compiled at the end of shifts, leading to a 24-hour lag in visibility for management.
* **Data Integrity Risks:** High human-error rates in manual entries resulted in inconsistent inventory levels and unreliable financial forecasting.

---

## 🛠️ The Solution: A Unified ERP Framework

We developed a **Single ERP Platform** designed to act as the "Single Source of Truth" for the entire production lifecycle.

### Core Features:
1.  **Strict Quota Management:** Hard-coded logic to set and monitor daily/monthly production ceilings.
2.  **Live Transaction Tracking:** Every movement is logged instantly, providing an audit trail for every SKU.
3.  **Automated Dashboards:** Visualizing complex production data into simple, actionable KPIs.

---

## 📈 Key Outcomes & Business Value

By shifting from manual logs to an automated ERP, the company achieved measurable operational improvements:

| Metric | Manual Process (Before) | Production Monitoring System (After) | Improvement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Visibility** | 24-Hour Delay | **Real-Time** | 100% Instant |
| **Quota Breaches** | Frequent / Unmonitored | **Zero (Auto-Blocked)** | High Risk Mitigated |
| **Reporting Time** | 2 Hours / Day | **Automated** | 100% Time Saved |
| **Accuracy** | ~85% (Estimate) | **99.9% (System Validated)** | Significant Reliability |

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture (High-Level)

The system is built on a modern stack to ensure high availability and low latency during peak production hours.

* **Frontend: React.js** Built with a focus on high-speed data entry and responsive data grids.
* **Backend: Springboot** Complex validation engines to calculate production limits against real-time stock levels.
* **Database: PostgreSQL** Storing high-frequency scan logs and production master data with strong relational integrity.
